## 2026-04-15 - [Specific Contextual Labels for Modals]
**Learning:** Generic 'Cerrar' labels on modal close buttons lack context for screen reader users, especially in complex views like Report Comparisons.
**Action:** Always provide context-specific aria-labels and titles (e.g. 'Cerrar comparación') rather than generic 'Cerrar' labels, particularly on icon-only buttons in complex modals.
